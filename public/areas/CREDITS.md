# Popular-area photos

The Explore screen's popular-area tiles use these neighbourhood photographs.
Each is unmodified apart from Wikimedia Commons resizing it to 500px wide.

- `akoka.jpg` — University of Lagos gate, Akoka. Photo by Ei'eke, CC BY 4.0.
  https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:University_of_Lagos_Gate.jpg
- `yaba.jpg` — Sabo, Yaba in the evening. Photo by Taiwo Ayanleye, CC BY-SA 4.0.
  https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Evening_View_at_Sabo_Yaba.jpg
- `surulere.jpg` — Bode Thomas, Surulere. Photo by Seun alone, CC BY-SA 4.0.
  https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Bode_Thomas_Surulere_Lagos.jpg
- `ikeja.jpg` — A road in Ikeja. Photo by Grace789, CC BY-SA 4.0.
  https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:View_of_a_road_along_Ikeja,_Lagos_State,_May_2023.jpg

Licence texts: CC BY 4.0 — https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/ and

To add a neighbourhood, drop a photo in this folder, point that area's `photo`
field in `src/pages/explore.tsx` at it, and add a line here.
